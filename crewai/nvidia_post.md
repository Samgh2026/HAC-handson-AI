### [NVIDIA's Latest Breakthroughs in Quantum Computing and AI](#)

September 2026 marks an exciting month for NVIDIA as it continues to cement its position at the forefront of quantum computing and artificial intelligence. Recently, researchers at the Jülich Supercomputing Centre achieved a record-setting simulation of fault-tolerant quantum computing. By leveraging NVIDIA's advanced technologies, they've developed models for complex quantum calculations, showcasing the immense potential of quantum systems in solving problems traditional computing struggles with. This monumental achievement highlights NVIDIA's commitment to exploring the nexus of AI and quantum computing.

In another groundbreaking development, NVIDIA has joined the European Commission’s AI in Science Enablers Initiative. This collaboration aims to provide open learning resources and computational support to advance scientific research across Europe. By harnessing NVIDIA's immense computing capabilities, the initiative seeks to empower researchers, fostering innovation and accelerating discoveries in various scientific fields. This move not only benefits researchers but also strengthens NVIDIA's reputation as a leader in applying AI to real-world challenges.

NVIDIA is also expanding its CUDA-Q platform to support fault-tolerant quantum computing better. The introduction of CUDA-Q Logical as a new orchestration layer allows developers to build and optimize quantum applications seamlessly. This innovation streamlines the integration of quantum computing capabilities into existing workflows, enabling developers to create powerful quantum applications efficiently. Such advancements further align with NVIDIA's vision of making quantum technologies more accessible to developers and researchers alike.

Additionally, NVIDIA has announced a significant expansion of AI supercomputers in Europe. With 35 new systems rolling out, NVIDIA's infrastructure is set to equip millions of researchers and innovators across the continent. These state-of-the-art AI systems promise to drive forward scientific discovery, further establishing NVIDIA's role as an indispensable ally in the global quest for knowledge. As NVIDIA continues to push the boundaries of technology, the world watches eagerly for the next wave of transformation in AI and quantum computing.
